# Write a customer proposal

1. Find out what the customer needs. Use their customer record if you have one. If not, ask.
2. Write exactly three sections, titled Need, Offer and Next step.
3. Label any price or date you can't confirm as "to be confirmed".
4. Show the draft to the employee. Do not send it.
